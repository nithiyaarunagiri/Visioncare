Our Story

From a Small Clinic to a Trusted Name.

VisionCare began in 2010 as a single-room eye clinic in London. Our founder, a practising optometrist, wanted to build a place where people could get honest eye care and frames that actually fit their face — not just the latest trend.

"We didn't set out to build a brand. We set out to help people see better — the rest followed."

Today, VisionCare has grown into a full optical store and eye care center, trusted by over 500 families. Every frame we stock, every lens we fit, and every eye test we run — it's all built on the same simple promise: clear vision, done right.

What Drives Us

Our Mission & Our Vision

Two guiding principles that shape everything we do — from the frames on our shelves to the care we give every customer.

Our Mission

To make expert eye care and quality eyewear accessible to everyone — with honest advice, transparent pricing, and frames that feel as good as they look. Every customer deserves to leave seeing better and feeling confident.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted name in eye care and eyewear — a place where clinical expertise and everyday style meet. We want to change how people think about their vision, one pair of glasses at a time.

Milestones

Our Journey So Far

From a single clinic room to a full optical store and eye care center — here's how VisionCare grew over the years.

2010
The Beginning

VisionCare opens as a single-room eye clinic in London, offering basic eye tests and a small selection of frames.

2014
First Store Expansion

We expand into a full optical store, adding designer frames and advanced lens-fitting equipment.

2018
Certified Eye Care Center

VisionCare becomes a certified eye care center, with on-site optometrists and diagnostic technology.

2022
500+ Happy Families

We cross 500 loyal customers and celebrate 12 years of helping people see better and look their best.

2026
A Growing Vision

Today, VisionCare continues to grow — with new collections, expanded services, and the same founding promise.
